KidsQ Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

In short: KidsQ collects no personal information. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking and no accounts, and it does not use the internet. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

About KidsQ

KidsQ is a game for young children to play and learn with letters, words and numbers. It is made by Qodbox. It is meant as a fun, educational tool. Children's screen time should be monitored by their parents.

Information we collect

None. KidsQ does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information about children or adults. It does not ask for names, ages, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, location or contacts.

What stays on your device

So that children can carry on where they left off, KidsQ keeps a few things on the device itself:

  • game progress, such as the word length and number levels reached;
  • the settings a parent chooses, such as sound, music and letter style.

This never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling KidsQ deletes it. A parent can also reset progress in the app's Settings.

Internet, ads and third parties

KidsQ does not ask the device for internet access, so it cannot send or receive anything over the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no social features, and it does not link children to websites or other apps.

Parents' area

Settings sit behind a parent gate (press and hold). From there a parent may choose to write to us; that opens the device's own email app, and anything sent is between the parent and us, outside KidsQ.

Children's privacy

KidsQ is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

Changes to this policy

If KidsQ ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
